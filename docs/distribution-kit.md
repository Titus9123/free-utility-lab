## Community-safe post angles

### MealPlanSheet

I made a free weekly meal planning page that helps turn meals into a grocery list. No signup, just a simple planner/checklist format. Useful if you plan groceries before shopping.

### BudgetReset

I made a free zero-based budget / bill organizer resource. It is browser-based, no signup, and meant for organizing monthly bills and savings goals before the month starts.

### MoveBudget

I made a free moving cost checklist and supplies planner for apartment moves. It covers more than rent: deposit, movers, boxes, utilities, furniture and buffer money.

### AIStackCost

I made a free AI subscription cost planning guide for comparing tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and role-specific tools before stacking monthly subscriptions.
