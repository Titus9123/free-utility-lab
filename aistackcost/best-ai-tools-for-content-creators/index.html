The shortlist, by category

Scripts, captions & ideas: ChatGPT or Claude, free tier or ~$20/mo (openai.com / anthropic.com). Best all-round starting point for any content format.
Thumbnails & cover art: Midjourney, from ~$10/mo (midjourney.com). Worth it once thumbnails or covers are a weekly task, not a one-off.
Social graphics & templates: Canva, free tier or Pro ~$13-18/mo (canva.com). Covers most creators' design needs without a separate design subscription.
Cited research for scripts/posts: Perplexity, free tier or Pro ~$20/mo (perplexity.ai). Useful when accuracy and sourcing matter for the content.
Long-form scripts at volume: Jasper, from ~$39-49/mo (jasper.ai). Only worth it at high weekly output volume — most creators don't need it.

How to pick without overpaying

Match one tool to your actual bottleneck, not to the full list. A creator who writes but doesn't design needs the assistant and skips Canva and Midjourney entirely; a visual creator who rarely writes long captions can skip the research tool. Reassess monthly — the tool that mattered most when you were starting out often isn't the one that matters once you're publishing consistently.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the creator AI tools budget planner?

This page is a curated shortlist of specific tools by category. The creator AI tools budget planner is the framework for deciding how much to allocate across those categories in the first place.

Which tool should a new creator try first?

Start on the free tier of a general assistant for scripts and captions, and Canva's free tier for graphics. Add a paid tool only once a specific task becomes a weekly bottleneck.

Is Midjourney worth it for creators who don't do visual art?

Usually only for thumbnail and cover art. If your content is mostly text or talking-head video, Canva or your editing software's built-in tools cover more of your actual needs.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Prices and plans change often — confirm current figures on each vendor's own pricing page before buying.
