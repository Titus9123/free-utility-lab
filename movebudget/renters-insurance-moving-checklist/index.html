When coverage needs to start

Renters insurance should typically be active on move-in day, not scheduled to start later — most landlords require proof of coverage before handing over keys, and your belongings are at the most risk during the move itself (in a truck, in transit, briefly unattended).

What to check before choosing a policy

  • Does it cover belongings while in transit, not just once they're in the unit?
  • What's the deductible, and does it make sense for what you actually own?
  • Does the landlord require a specific minimum liability coverage amount?
  • Can you bundle it with an existing auto or other policy for a lower rate?

Frequently asked questions

When should renters insurance coverage start?

On or before move-in day — most landlords require proof of active coverage before releasing keys, and belongings are at risk during the move itself, not just after unpacking.

Does renters insurance cover items damaged during the move?

Check the specific policy — coverage for belongings in transit varies by insurer, so ask directly rather than assuming standard coverage applies during the move.

Can renters insurance be bundled with another policy?

Often yes, with an existing auto or other insurance policy, which can lower the rate compared to buying a standalone renters policy.
