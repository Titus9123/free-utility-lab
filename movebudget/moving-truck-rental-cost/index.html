What actually drives truck rental cost

Truck size (studio vs 3-bedroom load), mileage if it's a one-way rental, and how many rental days you keep it are the three biggest cost drivers — more than the base daily rate advertised. A one-way rental across a long distance often costs several times a local round-trip rental for the same truck size.

Before you book

  • Confirm whether mileage is included or charged separately.
  • Ask about insurance/damage waiver cost — it's often not included in the advertised rate.
  • Book earlier in the month; end-of-month dates (common lease turnover) sell out and can cost more.
  • Check fuel policy — some require a full tank return, others charge a refuel fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is a one-way truck rental much more expensive than round-trip?

Often yes, especially over long distances — one-way rentals typically cost more per mile since the company has to reposition the truck. Compare both options directly with the rental company rather than assuming.

What size truck do I actually need?

Match it to your apartment size, not your total item count — a studio to one-bedroom move usually needs a smaller truck than people assume, while a full house needs more capacity than the smallest options offer.

Does truck rental insurance cost extra?

Usually yes — it's commonly quoted separately from the base daily rate, so factor it into the total cost comparison between rental companies, not just the advertised price.
