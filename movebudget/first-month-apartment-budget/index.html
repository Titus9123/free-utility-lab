First month apartment budget: how much cash do you need?

Your first month apartment budget is usually much larger than rent alone. A simple planning range is 2–4× monthly rent once deposit, fees, movers, utilities and essentials are included.

This is not financial advice. It is a planning checklist to avoid common move-in surprises.

2× rentlow-friction move
3× rentcommon renter budget
4× renthigher-cost move

What pushes the budget higher?

  • Large security deposit or broker/admin fees.
  • Long-distance movers or difficult building access.
  • New furniture, kitchen items and cleaning supplies.
  • Utility deposits, internet installation and insurance.

FAQ

Why does the range go as high as 4× rent?

The high end assumes a larger deposit, professional movers, a full furniture set and higher utility deposits — costs that stack when moving to a nicer unit or a farther distance, not typical for every move.

Can the first month be lower than 2× rent?

Yes, especially with an existing furniture set, a DIY local move, and a landlord who waives or reduces the deposit — 2× is a reasonable floor, not a guaranteed minimum.

Should the first-month target include the security deposit twice?

No — count the deposit once. It's easy to double-count it if it's already folded into a general "moving costs" estimate elsewhere in your planning.
