Beyond boxes: the supplies people forget to budget

Boxes get most of the attention, but tape (more than one roll — underestimating tape is nearly universal), bubble wrap or paper for fragile items, permanent markers for labeling, and furniture blankets or stretch wrap for larger pieces are the supplies that actually run out mid-pack. Budget for these as their own line item instead of assuming "a few rolls of tape" will cover a whole apartment.

How this differs from the box calculator

The moving box calculator estimates box count and size by room. This page covers everything else needed to actually pack those boxes — tape, wrap, labels and protective materials — which is easy to underestimate once the box count is sorted.

FAQ

How much tape does an average apartment move need?

More than most people expect — plan for multiple rolls per room rather than one roll total; running out of tape mid-pack is one of the most common last-minute supply runs.

Is bubble wrap necessary or can towels work instead?

Towels and clothing can cushion dishes and fragile items in a pinch, which also reduces how much bubble wrap you need to buy — a practical budget-saver, not just a packing hack.
