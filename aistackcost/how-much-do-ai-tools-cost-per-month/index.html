How much do AI tools cost per month?

A solo user might spend $20–$60 per month. A creator or operator can easily spend $80–$200. A small team can cross $300–$1,000 per month once seats and overlapping tools are included.

Why the jump between tiers is so steep

The cost doesn't scale with users, it scales with seats × tools. One person on one tool is $20/month; five people on three tools is $300+/month, even though each individual price looks reasonable. The fastest way to find real savings is comparing the seat count against actual weekly logins, not shopping for a cheaper plan tier.

FAQ

What does the average person actually spend on AI tools monthly?

A solo user with one general assistant typically spends $0-20/month. Add a design tool and the range moves to $30-40/month. Creators and small teams running several specialist tools commonly land between $80-250/month.

Why do costs jump so much between a solo user and a small team?

Because seats multiply: a $20/month tool becomes $100/month across five people, and teams typically run more specialist tools (design, automation, analytics) than a single freelancer needs.

What is the single biggest cost driver?

Paid seats nobody uses. Reviewing active seats against actual weekly usage typically finds more savings than switching to a cheaper plan tier.
