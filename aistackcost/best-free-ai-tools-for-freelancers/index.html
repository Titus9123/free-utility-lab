Stack preset library

Freelancer stack preset

Audience: solo freelancer

  • one general AI assistant
  • one design tool
  • one notes/project tool

Buying rule: Upgrade only when a paid feature saves billable time every week.

Small business stack preset

Audience: small business owner

  • one general AI assistant
  • one marketing/design tool
  • one automation or CRM helper

Buying rule: Avoid duplicate AI subscriptions until a workflow owner uses each tool weekly.

Agency stack preset

Audience: small agency

  • shared writing/research assistant
  • design tool
  • automation layer
  • meeting/documentation helper

Buying rule: Track per-seat cost and cancel overlapping tools after a two-week trial.

Methodology

Methodology: group tools by job-to-be-done, prefer free options until usage is proven, compare recurring cost, and remove overlapping subscriptions before upgrading.
