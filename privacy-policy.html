Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

1. What Free Utility Lab is

Free Utility Lab publishes free browser-based calculators, planners, checklists and generators. The tools are designed to work without signup and without asking for sensitive account numbers, passwords or private identifiers.

2. Information you type into tools

Most tool inputs are processed locally in your browser. We do not intentionally collect the numbers, lists or planning details you enter into free calculators and planners. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, government IDs, medical records or other sensitive information into these tools.

3. Usage and analytics

We may measure aggregate, non-sensitive usage events such as page views, tool starts, tool completions, copy clicks, print clicks and download clicks. These events are used to improve the tools and do not need your private result values.

4. Cookies and future advertising

The site may use essential cookies for basic functionality. If advertising is activated later, ad partners such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Where required, visitors may be shown consent choices before non-essential advertising cookies are used. No live ad network scripts are active at launch.

5. Third-party links

Some pages may link to official tool providers, app stores, reference pages or related resources. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact channel published with the current Free Utility Lab project or repository. Do not send secrets or passwords.
