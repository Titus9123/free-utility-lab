An agent's AI stack, role by role

Real estate work is mostly repeated writing and repeated visuals: listing descriptions, buyer/seller emails, neighborhood summaries, and social posts for every new listing. That's exactly what a general assistant plus a design tool covers, without needing a specialist real-estate-branded AI product.

Listing copy & client emails: ChatGPT or Claude, ~$20/mo (openai.com / anthropic.com). Draft descriptions from an address and feature list, then edit for accuracy.
Flyers, social posts, staging mockups: Canva Pro, ~$13-18/mo (canva.com). Templates built for real estate marketing are widely available inside Canva.
Market/neighborhood research: Perplexity, ~$20/mo (perplexity.ai), useful for pulling cited local data points to support a listing narrative — always verify against your MLS and local records.

Where AI should not be trusted

Never let an AI tool draft binding contract language, fair-housing-sensitive listing copy, or disclosure statements without a licensed review — use it for first-draft speed on marketing copy only, and always fact-check comparable sales data, square footage and legal details against your MLS and brokerage compliance rules.

Frequently asked questions

What AI tool should a real estate agent buy first?

A general assistant (ChatGPT or Claude, ~$20/month) for listing descriptions, buyer/seller emails and comparable-property summaries. It covers the widest range of daily tasks for the lowest cost.

Is a paid design tool worth it for listing photos and flyers?

Often yes once you're listing regularly. Canva Pro (~$13-18/month) covers flyers, social posts and virtual staging mockups faster than a free tier or a designer for routine listings.

Can AI replace a real estate photographer or MLS data?

No. AI tools help draft copy and design assets faster; they don't replace licensed MLS data feeds, professional photography or legal disclosure requirements.

Are prices guaranteed?

No. Prices change; verify current figures on each vendor's own pricing page before buying.
