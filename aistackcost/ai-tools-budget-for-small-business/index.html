A starter AI stack for a small business

Most small businesses need three roles covered, not ten tools: a general assistant for writing and research, a design tool for marketing and social content, and an automation tool that connects forms, emails and spreadsheets. Everything past that should be added only when a specific recurring task justifies it.

Cash-flow rules that prevent subscription creep

Put one named owner in charge of approving every new AI subscription, keep a single running list of what the business pays for and why, and put a recurring 90-day reminder on the calendar to cancel anything nobody has opened. Annual plans are cheaper per month but lock in a tool choice for a year — only commit to annual billing once a tool has proven its worth on the monthly plan first.

FAQ

What is a realistic monthly AI budget for a 10-person small business?

A common starting range is $100-250/month: one shared general assistant seat or two, one design tool seat, and one automation plan sized to actual task volume. Add more only when a specific role proves it saves paid time every week.

Should every employee get their own AI subscription?

No. Start with shared or role-based seats for the general assistant and design tool, and give individual seats only to people who use a tool daily as part of their job.

How do small businesses avoid AI subscription creep?

Put one person in charge of approving new AI subscriptions, review the full list quarterly, and cancel any tool that has not been used in the last 30 days.

Are the prices on this page guaranteed?

No. Prices and plans change often. Confirm current pricing on the vendor's own page before buying or renewing.
